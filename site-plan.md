## Home Page Structure

The home page opens with a direct value proposition: **“One vision. Every part of the backyard.”** The hero explains that TPC Eckroat coordinates turf, pools, concrete, structures, and complete outdoor environments across the Edmond and Oklahoma City area. Primary and secondary calls to action lead to the contact page and the click-to-call telephone URI.

The service overview follows the logo’s order—**Turf, Pools, Construction**—and links to focused pages. A proof section communicates construction experience since 2007, pool experience since 2017, and service across a roughly 60-mile radius without inventing customer metrics or testimonials. The page closes with a practical project-planning call to action.

## SEO Implementation

Each route will set a unique title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph metadata, and service-specific body copy. The website will include semantic headings, internal links, descriptive image alternative text, an XML sitemap, robots directives, and LocalBusiness/HomeAndConstructionBusiness JSON-LD with the verified phone, address, email, service area, and service categories.

The primary geographic language will be **Edmond and the Oklahoma City metro**, with Stillwater and nearby communities included naturally in service-area copy. Keyword use will remain conversational; pages will answer service intent rather than repeat phrases mechanically.

## Conversion and Accessibility Requirements

Every telephone link will use the full U.S. URI format `tel:+14055091988` so iOS and Android can initiate calls reliably. The header, mobile navigation, service pages, and final calls to action will expose clear contact paths. Buttons and links will preserve visible keyboard focus, mobile touch targets will be at least 44 pixels high, motion will respect reduced-motion preferences, and image overlays will maintain text contrast.

The footer will include an automatically calculated current year and the required credit: **Created by JRPMTS**, linked to `https://www.jrpmts.com`.
