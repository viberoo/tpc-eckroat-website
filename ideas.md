# Chosen Direction: Prairie Club Modernism

## Design Movement

The website follows **Contemporary Regionalism with private-club editorial influences**: disciplined typography and premium restraint paired with Oklahoma landscape colors, outdoor textures, and structural forms.

## Core Principles

1. **Quiet confidence over sales noise:** Headlines are direct, compositions are spacious, and proof is demonstrated through service depth rather than exaggerated claims.
2. **Landscape and structure in equal measure:** Water, turf, stone, concrete, wood, and built forms are treated as one coordinated outdoor environment.
3. **Asymmetry with control:** Offset imagery, side-aligned type, and architectural rules create movement without sacrificing clarity.
4. **Material credibility:** Texture, strong contrast, and project-scale photography make the business feel experienced and hands-on.

## Color Philosophy

The palette grows from the supplied logo rather than imposing a new identity. **Fairway green** signals turf, longevity, and regional landscape; **deep pool blue** conveys water and technical control; **limestone gray** represents concrete and construction; and **flag gold** is reserved for calls to action and precise moments of emphasis. Warm off-white prevents the site from feeling corporate or sterile.

## Layout Paradigm

The primary layout is a **club-journal composition**: a slim utility bar, confident left-weighted navigation, an offset hero with a tall photographic field, and sections that alternate between narrow editorial copy columns and oversized visual bands. Section numbers, vertical rules, and staggered content edges replace a centralized card grid.

## Signature Elements

1. **Flag-point accent:** A small gold triangular motif derived from the golf flag in the logo appears beside section labels and key links.
2. **Pool-line sweep:** Thin blue curved rules echo the wave shape inside the “P” and are used as separators or image accents.
3. **Material index labels:** Small uppercase labels such as “TURF / POOLS / CONSTRUCTION” function like architectural drawing annotations.

## Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel decisive and tactile. Buttons depress slightly on click, navigation underlines sweep in from the left, and project/service panels reveal useful details without ornamental motion. Every interaction should reinforce trust and ease of contact.

## Animation

Use short opacity-and-transform entrances between 180–260 ms with a strong ease-out curve. Hero copy enters in a restrained vertical sequence; service imagery receives a subtle scale shift on hover; the mobile menu slides from the right with no bounce. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`, never animate layout dimensions, and avoid continuous decorative motion.

## Typography System

Use **Barlow Condensed** for display headlines, section numbers, and strong service labels. Its engineered proportions reference construction signage and athletic/private-club graphics. Use **DM Sans** for body copy, navigation, and controls for clarity at all sizes. Headlines should be uppercase or title case with tight tracking; body copy remains sentence case with generous line height. Display sizes should be visibly stepped: hero 64–92 px desktop, section headings 42–60 px, service titles 28–36 px, body 17–19 px.

## Brand Essence

**Positioning:** Complete outdoor environments for homeowners across Edmond and the Oklahoma City metro, led by real construction experience and built around how each property is meant to be used.

**Personality:** Capable, grounded, meticulous.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are concise and confident, calls to action are practical, and microcopy sounds like an experienced builder speaking plainly. Avoid generic promises and inflated superlatives.

**Example headline:** “One vision. Every part of the backyard.”

**Example CTA:** “Plan your outdoor project.”

## Wordmark & Logo

Use the supplied TPC Eckroat logo as the ground-truth brand mark. Its internal symbols—the flag-shaped T, water-swept P, and concrete-textured C—should guide iconography and layout accents. For small contexts, use a simplified standalone flag-and-wave symbol with no text, maintaining the same green, blue, gray, and gold visual vocabulary.

## Signature Brand Color

**Eckroat Fairway — `#174D3A`** is the ownable anchor color. It should appear in navigation, section backgrounds, and major trust-building surfaces, while pool blue and gold remain supporting accents.

## Style Decisions

Eckroat Fairway `#174D3A` is the dominant brand field color. Pool blue is reserved primarily for water references, linework, active states, and limited emphasis rather than repeated full-width page fields.

Every page includes a material-credibility moment through project-scale photography or a field-note treatment tied to turf, water, concrete, stone, wood, grading, or outdoor structure.

The three service pages share one editorial system but express different material cues: turf uses green contour language, pools use precise blue waterline geometry, and construction uses limestone/concrete structural mass.
